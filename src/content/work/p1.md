This video presents the conceptual design of the management system developed during my internship at Tunisair's headquarters in Tunis as a web development technician. This project aims to centralize and facilitate administrative management within the company through several complementary modules. It includes user management, with a role and permissions system to secure and control access to various functionalities, service and department management to organize the internal structure, and leave request management covering all stages of the process (submission, validation, and history tracking). The system also includes a program and planning management module, providing better organization of activities and resources.
